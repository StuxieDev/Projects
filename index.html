Bi Kat

A little pixel kat that follows your pointer, on any site

The pixel kat from stuxie.dev that runs after your pointer, sits down when it catches up and naps if you leave it be, now for any site, like oneko. One script tag puts it on yours, and a userscript brings it to every site you visit. Pick the bi kat or one of the Ridgwell cats (Olly, Lily, Poppy or Dougal) with the kat picker, in stuxie.dev’s footer, on kat.stuxie.dev or in the userscript’s menu. There are oneko spritesheets of all five too. Original pixel art, with no images and no tracking.
